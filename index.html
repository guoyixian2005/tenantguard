Frequently Asked Questions

How can you tell if a paystub is fake?

Almost all online fake stub generators produce rounding discrepancies in statutory FICA taxes (Social Security must be exactly 6.2% and Medicare 1.45% of gross pay). Furthermore, PDF metadata routinely betrays design tools (Photoshop/Canva/Sejda) or known scam generation engines.

Does this violate the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA)?

TenantGuard is a document mathematical verification and forensic tool, not a consumer credit reporting agency. It verifies factual integrity and arithmetic consistency of documents submitted directly by applicants to assist in your independent diligence.

Is the applicant's data safe?

Yes. All files are processed in ephemeral memory and automatically purged from our systems within 24 hours. We never sell, share, or store applicant PII.
